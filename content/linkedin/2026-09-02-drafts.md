## Draft: the people building a better world, running themselves into the ground

I teach yoga and I run ultras. People usually assume those two pull in opposite directions.

They do not. Both are really about the same question, which is how far you can go without breaking. And in both, the answer turns out to be further than you would think, but only if the rest is built in from the beginning rather than bolted on once you are wrecked.

What I keep noticing is where that goes wrong.

Some of the most driven people I meet are working on things that genuinely matter. Climate, cities, health, making something better than they found it. And they will talk about sustainability all day, while working in a way that is not remotely sustainable.

I have done the same thing myself. I used to treat rest as something you earn by first running yourself into the ground.

So, a few questions I have been sitting with, and you might find them worth a minute too.

Is there anything in your week that is actually restful, on purpose, and often enough to count?

If there is, are you the first person to drop it the moment things get busy?

And is it treated as a nice extra, or as part of how the work gets done?

I have never wanted to talk anyone out of ambition. Big goals are not the problem. It is the quiet assumption that the only way to reach them is to keep going until something gives.

What does a sustainable version of your busy season actually look like?
