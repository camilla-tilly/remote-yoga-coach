## Draft: preventative rest

For years I trained seven days a week. Rest days weren't really a thing. I was tired all the time, but if I wasn't training I felt like I wasn't getting anywhere.

Then I started following a proper plan for my first ultra, Ultravasan, which is the Vasaloppet but running. It had rest days built in, so I started taking a full day off each week. Turns out that let me do more, not less. Check yourself before you wreck yourself, if you will.

I think a lot of us work the way I used to train. Push until you're wiped out, then hope a weekend fixes it.

What if we did it the other way? Something small, regular and non-negotiable, to find new energy before we've used it all up.

Do you have a way you actually build rest into your week?
