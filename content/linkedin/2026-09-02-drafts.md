For years I trained seven days a week. Rest days weren't really a thing. I was tired all the time, but if I wasn't training I felt like I wasn't getting anywhere.

Then I started following a proper plan for my first ultra, Ultravasan, running the Vasaloppet. It had rest days built into it, and I began taking a full day off each week. I discovered that enabled me to do more. Check yourself before you wreck yourself, if you will.

I think a lot of us work the way I used to train. Push until you're wiped out, then hope a weekend fixes it.

What if we did it the other way? Something small, regular and non-negotiable, to find new energy before we've used it all up.

Here's the hard part though. Most of us like that idea right up until the week gets intense, and then it's the first thing to go.

So be honest with yourself. When things get stressful, is rest the first thing you cancel? And if it is, was it ever really non-negotiable?
