## Draft: what if rest was preventative

For years I only rested once I was already exhausted. That was the whole system. Push until something forced me to stop, collapse for two days, call it a break.

Ultra running is what changed my mind about that, which I did not expect.

In a training plan the rest is not what you get afterwards. It is scheduled in from the start, on set days, and the hard sessions are built around it. You do not earn it by suffering first. It is the thing that makes next week possible.

I got that wrong for a long time. I assumed the rest days were the soft part of the plan.

In yoga we talk about effort and ease as two halves of the same thing. I could teach that quite happily for a while before I actually did it myself.

I think a lot of us work the way I used to run. We go until we are depleted and then try to recover. And recovering from depleted takes far longer than staying topped up would have.

So what I keep wondering is whether rest could be preventative instead of a reaction.

Not a big gesture. Not a wellness day after a brutal quarter. Something small and regular that is already in the calendar before anyone needs it.

Is there anything like that in your week, and does it stay there when things get busy?

I do not have this fully worked out. But the weeks where something is genuinely protected feel different from the weeks where I plan to rest once things calm down. Things never calm down.
