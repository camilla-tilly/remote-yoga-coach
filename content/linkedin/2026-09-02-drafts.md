## Draft: preventative rest

For years I trained seven days a week. Rest days weren't really a thing. I was tired all the time, but if I wasn't training I felt like I wasn't getting anywhere.

Then I started following a proper plan for my first ultra, 92 kilometres. It had rest days built into it, the same as the hard sessions.

So I took a full day off every week and really embraced the whole check yourself before you wreck yourself philosophy. Not because I wanted to do less, but because it meant I could do more.

That's when rest stopped being what I did after I'd hit a wall, and became the thing that kept me going.

I think a lot of us work the way I used to train. Push until you're wiped out, then try to rest your way back. It takes so much longer that way round.

What if we did it the other way? Something small and regular, already in the calendar, before anyone needs it.

Mine is a morning meditation and one day off a week. Both are in the diary. Both are the first things I drop when I get busy.

Do you have a way you actually build rest into your week?
