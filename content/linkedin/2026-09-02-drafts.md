## Draft: preventative rest

For years I trained seven days a week. A rest day was not really a concept for me. I was tired most of the time, but I felt like I was not achieving anything if I was not training.

What changed was following a proper structured plan for an ultra. Rest days are built into it from the start, because you cannot put that much out and give nothing back. It does not work. So I had to take a full day off every week, and I started paying attention to sleep and eating properly too. Not in a restrictive way. More that if I looked after myself, I could do more.

That was the shift for me. Rest stopped being the thing I did once I had run myself into the ground, and became the thing that let me keep going.

I think a lot of us work the way I used to train. We push until we are wiped out, and then try to rest our way back. It takes much longer that way round.

So I have been wondering what it looks like to do it the other way. Something small and regular that is already in the calendar, before anyone needs it.

For me that is a morning meditation and one full day off a week. Both are in the diary, and both are the first things I am tempted to drop when I get busy.

Do you have a way you actually build rest into your week?
