## Draft: effort and ease

I teach yoga and I run ultras. People usually assume those two pull in opposite directions.

They do not. Both keep bringing me back to the same thing, which is the balance between effort and ease, and learning to actually listen to what my body is telling me instead of overriding it.

That is not a soft idea. In yoga it is more or less the whole practice. Over a long distance it is the difference between still moving at hour eight and not.

What I keep noticing is where that balance quietly goes missing.

Some of the most driven people I meet are working on things that genuinely matter. Climate, cities, health, making something better than they found it. And they will talk about sustainability all day, while working in a way that is not remotely sustainable.

I have done exactly the same thing. I used to treat rest as something you earn, and only after running yourself into the ground first.

So, a few questions I have been sitting with, and you might find them worth a minute too.

Is there anything in your week that is genuinely restful, on purpose, and often enough to count?

If there is, are you the first person to drop it the moment things get busy?

And is it treated as a nice extra, or as part of how the work actually gets done?

I have never wanted to talk anyone out of ambition. Big goals are not the problem. It is the quiet assumption that the only way to reach them is to keep pushing until something gives.

What does a sustainable version of your busy season look like?
