## Draft 1: what I actually run, plainly

MEDIA: a candid photo from a session at A House or Bruce Studios, her
teaching or the room mid-session, not posed for camera.

Someone in HR asked me last week what I actually do, because "yoga teacher who works with remote teams" hadn't quite landed for her. Fair question, so here it is plainly.

I run live wellbeing sessions for remote and hybrid teams, over Teams or Zoom.

Three separate class types: meditation, breathing and chair yoga. A team can pick one, rotate between them, or combine all three depending on what the week needs.

Camera is always optional. Most people join with it off, and that's completely fine, nobody is performing for anyone else.

Every session is recorded, so a different time zone or a clashing meeting doesn't lock anyone out.

It's a flat monthly subscription per team, not per head, and it's just me teaching it live, not an app or a library of pre-recorded videos.

If you're the person who ends up owning wellbeing at your company and you're not quite sure where to start, that's exactly who this is for. Pricing and booking are on my website, linked from my profile.

## Draft 2 (alternative, same type): the one big class I say no to

MEDIA: a candid photo from a chair yoga or breathing session, room shot,
not staged.

I get asked fairly often to come in and run one big yoga class for a team. I usually say no, and I'll explain why.

One class is a nice afternoon. It isn't a habit, and habits are the entire point.

So what I actually offer is three separate, shorter class types instead: meditation, breathing and chair yoga, live over Teams or Zoom. A team can pick one, rotate through them, or run all three across the month.

Camera optional, always. Every session recorded, so nobody is excluded by time zone or a meeting that ran long.

It's one flat monthly subscription per team, whatever the headcount, not a one-off workshop and not an app.

If your team's wellbeing budget has mostly gone on single events that everyone enjoyed and nobody remembers by the next sprint, this is built to be the opposite of that. Pricing and how to book are on my website, linked from my profile.
