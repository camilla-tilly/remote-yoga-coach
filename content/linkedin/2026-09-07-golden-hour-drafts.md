## Draft

I nearly put these classes at six in the evening. That's the obvious slot, and it's the one I'd have booked myself a few years ago.

Then I thought about how often I actually made it to an evening class.

The problem with 18:00 isn't motivation. It's that by then the day has had eleven hours to go wrong. A meeting overruns, something lands at half four, someone needs one more thing before they log off. The class is the flexible item, so the class is what gives.

Nothing has gone wrong at seven in the morning. There's nothing to overrun yet. It's the one hour most of us can genuinely protect, which is why it's the hour that actually holds.

That isn't a discipline thing, it's a scheduling thing. If you put the good stuff where the day can reach it, the day will take it.

So I've built this autumn around that. Mondays and Wednesdays, seven to eight, finished and showered well before nine.

I keep thinking the same is true inside companies. The wellbeing sessions that survive a busy quarter are almost never the ones booked for the end of the day.
