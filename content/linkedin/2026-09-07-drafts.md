## Draft: why groups work

In my early twenties I taught group fitness classes. This is me somewhere around then.

I have been thinking about why I still prefer teaching groups, and it comes down to something fairly unglamorous.

People turn up.

Not because anyone is more disciplined in a group. Because someone is expecting them, at a set time, and that turns out to matter far more than motivation does.

I notice the same in my own practice. I find it much easier to meditate when I know other people are sitting at the same time. On my own I can talk myself out of it in about four seconds.

There is something else too. In a group you still get your own experience, it is just held inside a shared one. Nobody is watching you, and everyone is slightly out of their comfort zone at the same time, which is oddly relaxing.

That is the part I think gets lost with an app. The content is usually fine. What is missing is the set time, the other people, and someone actually holding it.

Fifteen years on from those classes, that has not changed at all.

What is the thing you always turn up to, and what makes it stick?
