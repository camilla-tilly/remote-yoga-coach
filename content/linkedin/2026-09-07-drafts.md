## Draft: why group training works

Why is it so much easier to train with other people?

I started teaching group fitness at 23 (here's me teaching BodyPump back then) and I've been curious about it ever since.

Same workout, same effort. Completely different odds that anyone actually turns up.

I think it comes down to three things.

You don't want to let people down. Most of us will let ourselves down long before we'll let someone else down, so committing to other people is how a lot of us end up committing to ourselves.

You don't have to decide anything. The time is set, someone else is leading, and you just show up. There's no negotiation with yourself at six in the morning.

And you're part of something bigger than you. Everyone in the room is doing the same hard thing at the same time, and that carries you through the bits you would have quit on your own.

Most habits don't die because we stop caring. They die because doing it alone means deciding all over again, every single time.

What have you only ever managed to keep up with other people?
