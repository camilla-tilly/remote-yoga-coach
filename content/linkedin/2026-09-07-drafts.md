## Draft: why habits stick with other people

Why is it so much easier to keep a habit going when other people are involved?

I started teaching group fitness at 23 (here's me teaching BodyPump back then) and I've been curious about it ever since.

I think it comes down to three things.

You don't want to let people down. Most of us will let ourselves down long before we'll let someone else down, so committing to other people is how a lot of us end up committing to ourselves.

You don't have to decide anything. By the end of a working day we've all made hundreds of small decisions, and there isn't much left over for deciding whether to do the thing that's good for us. In a group the time is set, someone else is leading, and there's nothing to weigh up. You just show up.

And you're part of something bigger than you. Everyone is doing the same thing at the same time, and that carries you through the bits you'd have quit on your own.

It works the same whether it's a workout, a yoga class, or ten minutes of breathing. On your own it needs a decision every single time. With other people, it just happens.
