## Draft: why it is easier with other people

Why is it so much easier to do anything with other people?

I can talk myself out of a solo meditation in about four seconds. If I know a few others are sitting at the same time, I just do it. Same practice, completely different odds.

Running's the same. Alone in the dark in February, it's a negotiation. With someone waiting for you, it isn't.

I started teaching group fitness at 23 and I've been curious about this ever since. This is one of the rooms I taught in.

Part of it is obvious. Someone's expecting you at a set time, so not turning up has a cost.

But I think there's more to it. In a group you still get your own experience, it's just held inside a shared one. Nobody's really watching you, and everyone's a bit outside their comfort zone at once, which is oddly relaxing.

And you don't have to decide anything. The time is set, someone else is leading, you just show up.

That might be the whole thing. Most habits don't die because we stop caring. They die because they need a decision every single time.

What have you only ever managed to keep up with other people?
