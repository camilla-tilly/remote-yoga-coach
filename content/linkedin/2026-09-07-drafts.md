## Draft: why groups work

You're not bad at sticking to things. You're just doing them on your own.

I can talk myself out of a solo meditation in about four seconds. But if I know other people are sitting at the same time, I just do it. Same practice, completely different odds.

In my early twenties I taught group fitness classes. This is me somewhere around then.

Fifteen years later I've stopped being surprised by it. People turn up for a class in a way they don't turn up for themselves. Not because anyone's more disciplined in a group, but because someone's expecting them, at a set time.

There's something else too. In a group you still get your own experience, it's just held inside a shared one. Nobody's watching you, and everyone's a bit outside their comfort zone at once, which is weirdly relaxing.

That's the bit I think apps miss. The content is usually fine. What's missing is the time, the other people, and someone actually holding it.
