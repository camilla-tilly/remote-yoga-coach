POST TEXT:

Why is it so much easier to keep a habit going when other people are involved?

I started teaching group fitness classes when I was 23 (here's me teaching BodyPump back in the day). What struck me even then was the power of the group.

I think that power comes down to three things:

1. You turn up for other people. Most of us keep a promise to someone else more easily than a promise to ourselves, so committing to a group ends up being a way of committing to yourself.

2. You don't have to decide anything. A workday involves hundreds, if not thousands, of small decisions, and decision fatigue is real. There is something genuinely lovely about turning up somewhere and being told what to do.

3. You're part of something shared. Everyone is doing the same thing at the same time, and that pulls you through the bits you'd have skipped alone. It stops being your discipline and becomes a shared experience.

It works the same whether it's a book club, a choir or a guided meditation.

Have you managed to build habits on your own, or do they stick better when other people are involved?
